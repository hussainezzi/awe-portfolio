01
The problem

Design intent dies in translation.

  • The client describes a feeling — warm, open, not hotel-like. Not a drawing.
  • The requirement engineer interprets it, draws it, sends it back.
  • The client still can't read the drawing, so the loop starts again.
  • Every loop costs a week, a revision fee and sometimes the project.
FloorplanProblem
02
The insight

Clients don't buy square metres.
They buy what they can picture.

The gap isn't design skill. It's the interface between the client's words and the engineer's model. Fix the interface, and the whole project speeds up.
